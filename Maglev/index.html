Educational magnetic levitation case study with affordable components

This work presents the design, implementation, and testing of an educational magnetic levitation system in a hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) configuration. The proposed design allows the implementation of a wide range of controllers in an HIL, leveraging the power of available control toolboxes. The implementation is based on standard electronic components such as operational amplifiers, bistables, Hall sensors, and passive and active electrical components. Testing and validation experiments were carried out to evaluate the proposed setup. The results of these investigations are presented in the second part of the work, while the code and models used are available online at tamaslevente.github.io.
